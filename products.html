Frequently Asked Questions

How long does protection last?Up to four hours of protection per application.

Covexall forms a microscopic shield that bonds to your skin and keeps working after it dries. Reapply after washing hands or after four hours.

What does “kills 99.9% on contact” mean?It means Covexall kills 99.9% of germs on hands and surfaces in seconds.

In independent laboratory testing, Covexall achieved a complete kill of 11 bacterial species within 60 seconds.

Is Covexall safe for kids?Yes! It's water-based, alcohol-free, and non-irritating—made for all ages.

Keep out of eyes and mouth, as with any hand product.

Can I use it on sensitive skin?Yes. Covexall is non-irritating and gentle on skin.

No alcohol means no sting, no cracking and no drying, even with frequent use.
