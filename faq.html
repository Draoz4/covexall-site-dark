What is Covexall Hand Sanitizer?

A water-based, alcohol-free hand sanitizer that kills 99.9% of germs on contact and keeps protecting for up to four hours per application.

Does Covexall kill germs on contact?

Yes. In independent laboratory testing, Covexall achieved a complete kill of 11 bacterial species within 60 seconds.

How is Covexall different from other hand sanitizers?

Most sanitizers evaporate in minutes. Covexall bonds to your skin and keeps working for up to four hours—with no alcohol, no sting, and no drying.

What germs is Covexall effective against?

COVID-19 (SARS-CoV-2), MRSA, C. diff (including spores), E. coli, and more. See the Proof page.

How long does Covexall protect for?

Up to four hours of protection per application.

Is Covexall alcohol-free?

Yes. Covexall is 100% alcohol-free.

Is Covexall safe for children?

Yes. It's water-based, alcohol-free, and non-irritating—made for all ages. Keep out of eyes and mouth.

Does Covexall dry out your skin?

No. With no alcohol, Covexall won't crack, sting, or dry your hands, even with frequent use.

Can I use Covexall on my face?

Covexall is formulated for hands. Avoid contact with eyes and mouth.

Where is Covexall made?

Covexall is made in the USA in an FDA cGMP-compliant facility.

How do I apply Covexall?

Apply a small amount, rub hands together until dry, and you're protected. Reapply after washing hands or after four hours.

Does it leave a sticky residue?

No. Covexall dries fast with no sticky feel or strong smell.
